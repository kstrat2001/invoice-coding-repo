# Copperleaf Cycle Works: cost codes and coding policy

Accounts payable codes every vendor invoice to one cost code before it is
paid. Copperleaf Cycle Works is a bike shop: it sells bikes, parts and
clothing, and it services customers' bikes in its own workshop. Use only the
codes below.

## Coding policy

1. Code what was bought, not who sold it. A vendor we usually buy one kind of
   thing from can send an invoice for something else.
2. Equipment and tools we keep and use are 1500 when a single item costs
   $2,500.00 or more, and 6450 when it costs less. Use one item's unit price,
   before tax and shipping, never the line total or the invoice total.
3. Freight, shipping and handling charged on goods we buy are part of those
   goods: code them with the goods, even when a carrier bills the freight on
   its own invoice. 6550 is only for shipping we pay to send goods to
   customers.
4. Lubricants, cleaners and similar supplies are 6460 when the workshop uses
   them up, and 5100 when we buy them in retail packs to sell; an invoice for
   stock to sell says so ("for resale" or "retail stock").
5. An expense whose covered period starts more than 90 days after the invoice
   date is 1400, whatever kind of expense it is. Software and subscriptions
   (6300) are the one exception: they are always 6300, however far ahead they
   are billed.
6. A refundable deposit is 1300, whoever it is paid to. It is not rent and not
   rental.
7. Repairs are coded by what was repaired: the premises 6400, our own
   equipment 6410, the shop's van 7450, and a customer's bike 5300.
8. Each invoice gets exactly one code. Every invoice in the batch has lines of
   only one kind (apart from freight and tax on those lines), so one code
   always fits the whole invoice.
