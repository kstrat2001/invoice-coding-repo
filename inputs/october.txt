# Copperleaf Cycle Works: cost codes and coding policy

Accounts payable codes every vendor invoice to one cost code before it is
paid. Use only the codes below.

## Cost codes

| Code | Name | Use it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1300 | Deposits | Refundable deposits we pay, such as a lease security deposit |
| 1400 | Prepaid expenses | Expenses paid more than 3 months before the period they cover (see rule 4) |
| 1500 | Shop equipment | Equipment we keep and use, when a single item costs $2,500.00 or more |
| 5100 | Parts and accessories for resale | Parts, tires, tubes and accessories bought to sell or to fit on customers' bikes |
| 6100 | Rent | Rent for the shop and storage space |
| 6150 | Equipment rental | Renting equipment we do not own |
| 6200 | Utilities | Power, water, gas, internet and phone |
| 6300 | Software and subscriptions | Software, apps and online services, however they are billed |
| 6400 | Repairs and maintenance | Repairing the building or the equipment we own |
| 6450 | Small tools | Tools and equipment we keep and use, when a single item costs less than $2,500.00 |
| 6500 | Office supplies | Paper, printer ink, stationery and other office consumables |
| 6550 | Shipping and postage | Shipping we pay to send goods to customers |
| 6600 | Advertising and marketing | Ads, printing for promotions, sponsorships |
| 6700 | Professional fees | Accounting, legal and consulting services |
| 6800 | Insurance | Insurance premiums for the period they cover |

## Coding policy

1. Code what was bought, not who sold it. A vendor we usually buy one kind of
   thing from can send an invoice for something else.
2. Equipment we keep and use is 1500 when a single item costs $2,500.00 or
   more, and 6450 when it costs less. Use the item's own price, before tax and
   shipping.
3. Freight, shipping and handling charged on a purchase are part of what was
   bought: code them with the goods, never to 6550. 6550 is only for shipping we
   pay to send goods to customers.
4. An expense paid more than 3 months before the period it covers is 1400.
   Software and subscriptions are the exception: they are always 6300, however
   far ahead they are billed.
5. A refundable deposit is 1300, whoever it is paid to. It is not rent and not
   an expense.
6. Each invoice gets exactly one code. Every invoice in this batch has lines of
   only one kind, so one code always fits the whole invoice.
